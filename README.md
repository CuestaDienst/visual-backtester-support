# Backsight – Support

Backsight (Setup Backtester) is a Windows app for backtesting chart setups: you draw Entry, Stop Loss and Take Profit on the chart,
and Backsight looks for similar situations in the market's past and shows how the trade would have played out there.

**Contact:** cuestadev@proton.me – we usually answer within a few days.

## Report a problem

In the app: **Settings → Report a bug** (or the **?** button at the top). Describe what happened; technical information is shown
before sending and can be left out. You can also send the report by email instead.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit
- Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (included in Windows 11 and current Windows 10; Backsight tells you if it is missing)
- An internet connection to Binance's public market data. Binance is not available in every country (for example the United States);
  where it is blocked, Backsight cannot load charts or market data and says so.

## Frequently asked

**Where does the market data come from?** From Binance's public market data (Spot, USDⓈ-M Futures and stock perpetuals).
No Binance account or API key is needed.

**Why does the first backtest of a market ask to download data?** Backtesting needs the market's full price history on your PC
(about 100 MB for BTCUSDT). Viewing the chart does not.

**How much disk space and memory does it use?** You decide in Settings: *Storage limit* (the oldest cached market data is removed
first; your strategies and results are never deleted) and *Memory limit for analyses*.

**How do I remove all data?** Settings → Clear Storage removes the downloaded market data; uninstalling Backsight removes everything.

**Purchases and refunds?** Backsight is sold through the Microsoft Store. Refunds and payment questions are handled by Microsoft under its Store policies.

**Is this investment advice?** No. Backsight shows what happened in similar situations in the past; that does not guarantee future
results, and results exclude fees, slippage and funding.
