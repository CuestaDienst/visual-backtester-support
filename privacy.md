# Privacy Policy – Visual Backtester

*Effective date: October 5, 2026*

Visual Backtester is a desktop application for analysing historical
market data. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app has no user accounts,
no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your computer only:

- your settings, layouts and drawings
- your saved analysis results
- downloaded historical market data (price candles)

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can
delete the downloaded market data at any time via
Settings → Clear Storage, and all app data by uninstalling the app.

## Connections to third parties

To display charts and run analyses, the app downloads public market
data directly from Binance (for example api.binance.com,
fapi.binance.com and data-api.binance.vision). These requests contain
no personal data from the app. As with any internet connection,
Binance can see your IP address. Binance's own privacy policy applies
to these requests.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this
page with a new effective date.
