# Privacy Policy – Backsight

*Effective date: October 10, 2026*

Backsight (Setup Backtester) is a desktop application for analysing historical
market data. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data automatically. The app has no user
accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. The
only data that reaches us is a bug report you choose to send (see below).

## Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your computer only:

- your settings, layouts and drawings
- your saved analysis results
- downloaded historical market data (price candles)

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can
delete the downloaded market data at any time via
Settings → Clear Storage, and all app data by uninstalling the app.

## Connections to third parties

To display charts and run analyses, the app connects directly to
Binance's public market data services only:

- data-api.binance.vision and data-stream.binance.vision (spot market data)
- fapi.binance.com and fstream.binance.com (futures and stock market data)

These requests contain no personal data. They identify the app by name
and version, and as with any internet connection, Binance can see your
IP address. Binance's own privacy policy applies to these requests.

Apart from a bug report you choose to send (see below), the app does not
contact any other servers.

Backsight runs on Microsoft WebView2, a Windows component
provided by Microsoft, which may have its own update and diagnostic
connections governed by Microsoft's privacy policy.

## Bug reports (voluntary)

In the app you can send a bug report (Help "?" or Settings → Report a
bug). Only when you press Send, the following is transmitted to our
report service, which runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers):

- the description you typed,
- your email address, only if you enter one (to answer you),
- technical information, shown to you before sending and optional:
  app version, Windows version, the selected market and timeframe,
  and the last error messages shown in the app (file paths removed).

The report is stored as an entry in a private repository on GitHub that
only we can access, and is used only to fix problems and, if you gave
an email address, to reply. Our report service does not store your IP
address; it only uses it briefly, in hashed form, to limit the number of
reports. Cloudflare and GitHub process the data on our behalf under their
own privacy policies. If you prefer, you can send the report by email
instead; then your email provider applies. To have a report deleted,
write to cuestadev@proton.me.

Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.
